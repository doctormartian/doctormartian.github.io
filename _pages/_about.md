## About me

I am **Doctor Martian**, a Year 1 student of the **Diploma in Clinical Medicine (DCM)** at **Fort Portal College of Health Sciences**, Uganda.

I am building a foundation in clinical sciences while developing the soft skills that good clinicians need: clear communication, teamwork, leadership, empathy, and professionalism.
