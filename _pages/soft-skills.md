Soft skills are essential for safe, respectful clinical practice. These are areas I am actively developing during DCM Year 1.

## Leadership
Founding and leading student projects (Martian Project, Stakc).

## Communication
Clear explanation, listening, and health education with peers.

## Teamwork & Collaboration
Working with fellow DCM students on shared academic and project goals.

## Problem Solving
Applying clinical reasoning and practical innovation to learning challenges.

## Time Management
Balancing coursework, clinical foundations, and project work.

## Empathy & Professionalism
Treating patients and colleagues with dignity, honesty, and accountability.

---

These skills will be refined throughout training and practice.
